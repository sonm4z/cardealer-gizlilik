1. Who we are

Car Dealer Simulator is a game published under the name Zipzip Games and made by a family together with their children. For any question about this policy, contact us at zipzipgames12@gmail.com.

2. What information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The game does not connect to our own servers, there are no user accounts, and there is no chat or sharing with other players.

Your game progress (money, cars, missions, collection, records, settings) is kept only in a save file on your device. This file is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data also deletes this save.

3. In-app purchases

The game offers optional purchases: a VIP membership, a sales representative and packs of in-game money. Payments are taken and processed entirely by your device's app store: Google Play on Android, Apple's App Store on iPhone. We never see or store your card or payment details. How this information is handled is described in the Google Privacy Policy and the Apple Privacy Policy.

So that the same purchase is never granted twice, the purchase's transaction number is kept only in the save file on your device.

To guard against accidental taps, the game shows a confirmation window with the product name and price before every purchase ("Buy" / "Cancel"); if "Cancel" is chosen, no purchase starts. The game itself does not ask a separate parent question. The payment is then taken on the store's own screen and goes through the store's authentication. On Android: you can require a password or fingerprint for every purchase in your Google Play account (Google Play > Settings > Authentication), and if your child's account is managed with Google Family Link, purchases also follow the parental approval settings you chose in Family Link. On iPhone: the payment is confirmed with Face ID, Touch ID or your Apple Account password; if your child is in your Family Sharing group with Ask to Buy turned on, you must approve every purchase, and you can also turn off in-app purchases completely in Screen Time.

4. Third-party components

The game is made with the Unity engine by Unity Technologies. In-app purchases (Google Play and the App Store) are handled by Unity's purchasing component (Unity IAP). When a purchase is made, this component may send Unity technical information that does not directly identify you, so that the purchase system works reliably: for example device model, operating system (Android or iOS) version, language setting, app version, the purchased product and the transaction number. This information does not include your name, e-mail address, location or advertising ID.

Unity Analytics, crash reporting and ad services are turned off in the game, and Unity is told that there is no consent for ads or analytics, so the advertising ID is never read. For Unity's data practices see the Unity Privacy Policy.
